Keep bundled source notices and the license file when redistributing this dependency. Local experiments and their dependencies are excluded from this repository package.

## Shaders, mathematical references, and imported media

Preserve any author/source notices in effect files. Mathematical family names identify techniques and inspiration, not ownership of third-party implementations. A complete provenance review of all preexisting shaders is separate from this documentation pass.

Shadertoy API imports can carry author, source URL, and license metadata. Keep that information with exported projects and follow the imported shader's terms. Uploaded audio, images, and videos retain their original rights; selecting them in the app does not change those rights.
